- Manager page access is governed by public.page_permissions (page_key = route URL) checked in the _authenticated layout guard and sidebar via src/lib/page-permissions.ts; admins bypass. Why: admin assigns per-page access.
- Hand-cash/income-expense totals all come from fetchCashSummary in src/lib/cash-queries.ts; any new money table must be added there. It and fetchCashHistory read every row page by page (rowsOf → fetchAllRows), as does the AI snapshot (allRows), so no total stops at the 1000-row API limit; every collection counts as cash in, a contract advance too. Why: one consistent account across dashboard and reports.
- Staff (daily workers, engine mestri, managers) share the workers table by role; burn mestri (পুড়াই মেস্তুরি) is a sardar with kind='burn'. Earnings come only from src/lib/salary-accrual.ts: daily workers are present by default: every day in the same window as salary counts at the wage of that day unless a worker_attendance row marks it absent (present=false → 0) or half (is_half_day → 0.5) (dailyEarned/dailyDays; the attendance UI stores only these exceptions and a daily worker who leaves needs a leave_date); salary (salaryEarned) accrues inside the selected season from the later of join_date/season start to the earliest of today/leave_date/season end (Dhaka dates); sardarEarned counts kacha_brick_entries production + load + unload (not damage) plus sardar_work_entries, or salary (with pay_rates history) for burn. Earlier seasons' dues live in opening_balances (worker_id links a worker's). Paying a daily worker or staff member always goes through payWorker (src/lib/worker-pay.ts): last season's unpaid wage (opening_payments) is settled first, only the rest is a worker_payments row; the expense form and the AI both use it, and the staff pages show "+ গত বছরের" under the due. Sardars are different: a sardar payment stays this season's pay/dadan and only goes to last season's due when the user picks "পূর্বের দেনা" in the form. Why: payments are season-scoped, so earnings must be too; the owner wants old dues cleared before this season's pay.
- Global React Query defaults (staleTime 30s, refetchOnWindowFocus true) and router preload 'intent' live in src/router.tsx; a MutationCache onSuccess there invalidates all queries (and clears the AI cache) after any useMutation succeeds, so writes made outside useMutation must call invalidateQueries themselves. Why: instant page switching.
- AI assistant writes live in saveAiActionToDatabase in src/services/gemini.ts; it runs in the browser only after the user taps "খাতায় সেভ" in chat, and inserts into the same tables the pages use (worker/staff pay = WORKER_PAYMENT → payWorker, see below). Before a save card is shown, checkEntryParty matches the entry's person against the snapshot and the names in the reply/message, fills the id, moves a staff member from SARDAR_PAYMENT to WORKER_PAYMENT, and asks instead when unsure. Why: AI entries must appear everywhere like manual ones, and the model can write one name in its reply and another in data.
- One head list: src/lib/money-heads.ts (MONEY_HEADS) names every money head once (label, group, icon, colour): in (ইট বিক্রির টাকা, গত বছরের পাওনা আদায়, মালিকের বিনিয়োগ, ঋণ নেওয়া, দেওয়া ঋণ ফেরত), costs in four groups (শ্রমিক ও বেতন, কাঁচামাল, যন্ত্র ও ভাড়া, অন্যান্য খরচ) and "move" heads that are not costs (সম্পদ কেনা, মালিকের উত্তোলন, ঋণ দেওয়া, নেওয়া ঋণ পরিশোধ, পূর্বের দেনা পরিশোধ). expenseHead() maps an expenses.category (anything unknown, also AI free text, is আনুষাঙ্গিক) and supplierHead() a supplier's material_type to a head; fetchCashHistory tags every line with key/head/detail/note/locked. The forms, cash book, reports, dashboard and AI use these names; the spelling is সরদার (সর্দার only in AI name matching). Why: the same money must never show under two names.
- Two money forms with one layout: money out goes through ExpenseEntryDialog (src/components/expense-entry.tsx, every non-"in" head incl. owner salary/withdrawal, old dues, loans given/repaid) and money in through IncomeEntryDialog (src/components/income-entry.tsx). Shared pieces (Section, Chips, BalanceStrip, HeadTiles) are in money-form-parts.tsx. Every new entry on the cash book, dashboard, purchases page and its folders, suppliers, collections, staff pages, owners, loans and opening balances opens one of these; the old per-page dialogs remain only to edit a saved row (and the brick-against-advance dialog on opening balances, which is not cash). Each head: who (with the running due), what, bill, paid now, a strip আগে বাকি + বিল − দিলেন = পরে বাকি, date, note; a section is left out when a head has none. It writes the same tables as before. Folders only add people and show ledgers. Shared folder settings (contract configs, veku note format) live in src/components/contract-config.ts so there is no import cycle. Why: an entry must look the same wherever it is written.
- Material/parts expenses go to purchases + supplier_payments (not expenses); exception: parts bought without a shop name, fully paid, go to expenses as "যন্ত্রাংশ / মালামাল — <item>" (a part left due needs a shop name), and the purchases page reads them back into its other folder. Seller lists and name matching skip CONTRACT_TYPES; a new seller gets material_type = the chosen catalog item (raw) or 'ইঞ্জিন মালামাল'/the chosen parts item. Why: they must show on the supplier/material page.

- Supplier dues can be settled with bricks via the RPC settle_supplier_with_bricks (admin only): it creates an approved challan, a matching collection (method "ইট দিয়ে সমন্বয়") and a supplier payment (method "bricks"), linked by sales_entry_id with ON DELETE CASCADE. Why: brick sale and full expense both count while hand cash stays unchanged; deleting the challan undoes all three.
- Purchase catalog (raw_materials): item_group raw|other picks the folder, pricing unit|total|flex sets the entry form, pay_mode credit|cash decides storage: credit -> purchases (+ supplier_payments), cash (বিদ্যুৎ বিল, the form's own head) -> expenses with category = item name, which the purchases page reads back into its history. Why: bills never stay due and must hit the cash book once.
- Profit has one source: fetchProfitLoss in src/lib/pl-queries.ts (accrual) — /income-expense (লাভ-ক্ষতি), the season and yearly reports (opts.allSeasons for other seasons) all read it; nothing else computes a profit. Every cost is a PLLine tagged with a money-heads key (byHead, byGroup), so the page shows the four groups and a head opens its lines and its ledger (headLink). Purchases count at bill value on purchase_date (raw vs parts by catalog item_group, contract items by name), supplier_payments are cash only and are not counted again; labour is counted by work done, not paid (salary-accrual.ts rules: sardar kacha/load/unload amounts and work entries on their dates, burn sardars, staff and owner salary day by day via salaryDays, daily workers via dailyDays; one PLLine per person per month), while sardar/worker payments, dadan and owner salary taken are cash only (dues show on /dues); assets are reported apart (assetLines). Who owes what lives on /dues (src/lib/dues.ts buildDues from the AI snapshot; the dashboard tiles open it); the snapshot reads supplier bills/payments and loans all-time. Supplier dues (fetchSupplierDues) are all-time, not season-scoped; the suppliers page table, the purchases page's "মোট বাকি" card and the expense form's running due use the same all-time rule (the purchases page's bought/paid cards and the suppliers payment-history tab are this season). Hand cash still comes only from fetchCashSummary. Why: material bought on credit is a real cost even before it is paid.
- seasons has a CHECK (end_date > start_date) and the seasons form blocks overlapping active seasons. Why: a reversed range makes every sdb query return nothing.
- Production ledger: mills (মিল ১-৪) hold the current sardar and each kacha entry keeps its own mill_id/sardar_id; per-thousand rates live in production_rates (production|load|unload, effective_from) and only pre-fill new entries; an unload is saved by the RPC save_unload as one kacha_brick_entries 'unload' wage line plus production_entries grade rows linked by unload_entry_id (ON DELETE CASCADE). Why: a sardar or rate change must never rewrite past entries, and unload wages, stock and the "পাকা বের" counts all come from one entry.
- Wage/salary history lives in pay_rates (worker_id or sardar_id, amount, effective_from); earnings use rateAt/salaryEarned/dailyEarned in salary-accrual.ts, falling back to workers.daily_wage/monthly_salary or sardars.monthly_salary when a person has no history. Forms that change a wage must write a pay_rates row with the chosen start date. Why: editing a wage must not reprice days already worked.
- Kiln weather: src/lib/kiln-weather.ts fetches the Kapasia Open-Meteo forecast and turns it into signals by KILN_WEATHER_RULES (red: rain within coverLeadHours, thunderstorm, gusts; orange: rain later today, tonight, tomorrow; yellow: heat, fog) plus a drying-day rating. The admin dashboard shows red/orange via WeatherSignalBanner (toast + vibration once an hour for red while the app is open); the full view is /weather (admin only, not in PAGES). Phone notifications: pg_cron runs every 30 min, calling the Edge Function weather-alerts (x-cron-secret from app_secrets), which sends Web Push to push_subscriptions (admins only) and logs to weather_alert_log (red at most every 3 h, day and night; orange once a day 06-22; 6 am summary). VAPID keys are created by the function and kept in app_secrets (service role only). public/sw.js shows the notifications. supabase/functions/weather-alerts/kiln-weather.ts must stay an exact copy of src/lib/kiln-weather.ts (redeploy after changing it). Why: green bricks in the field must be covered before rain, and one rule set keeps the banner and page consistent.
- Contract folders on /purchases (src/components/contract-folder.tsx, configs LAND_CONTRACT ?f=land, CLEANER_CONTRACT ?f=clean and VEHICLE_CONTRACT ?f=vehicle): a person is a suppliers row with material_type = cfg.type ('জমি ভাড়া' / 'পরিচ্ছন্ন কর্মী' / 'গাড়ি ভাড়া'), what they are owed (season rent, the sweeper's season contract, or a vehicle job) is a purchases row with item_name = cfg.item, money given (e.g. weekly sweeper pay) is supplier_payments. Vehicle rent (cfg.trips) is per trip: quantity = trips, unit 'ট্রিপ', unit_price = rate per trip, and money paid on the spot is a supplier_payments row on the same day. Contract people are hidden from the suppliers page and the material seller list; vehicle owners are often customers too (same name, separate rows). A previous-season due is a bill dated the day before the season starts (note starts "পূর্বের বকেয়া"), so it adds to the due but not to this season's cost. Dues are all-time. The purchases page's raw/other folders and totals exclude CONTRACT_ITEMS/CONTRACT_TYPES; fetchCashHistory labels these payments "জমি ভাড়া পরিশোধ" / "পরিচ্ছন্ন কর্মী পরিশোধ" / "গাড়ি ভাড়া পরিশোধ"; fetchProfitLoss reports costs.landRent, costs.cleaning and costs.vehicleRent (costs.vehicle is the unused vehicle_expenses table). To add another such folder, add a config to CONTRACTS and the same three labels. Why: no new money table, so hand cash, cash book, P&L and dues already count it.
- Owners (/owners): investment and withdrawal are capital, read all-time (not season-scoped); a withdrawal is not a cost. With two or more owners each card shows a salary part: the monthly rate history is pay_rates with owner_id (exactly one of worker_id/sardar_id/owner_id), earned salary accrues in the season via salaryEarned, and salary taken is owner_transactions txn_type 'salary' (CHECK invest|withdraw|salary). Salary taken leaves hand cash: fetchCashSummary breakdown.ownerSalary, cash book head "মালিকের বেতন", fetchProfitLoss byHead.ownerSalary counts the salary earned (by month), not what was taken. With one owner the salary part is hidden unless that owner already has salary history; stop a salary with a 0 rate from a date. Why: owners' salary must reduce profit and hand cash, never their investment.
- Fixed assets (the kiln's share of a jointly owned excavator, a power line, a transformer) are expenses rows whose category starts with ASSET_PREFIX "সম্পদ — " (cash-queries.ts, isAssetCategory; cash-book head "সম্পদ"). They leave hand cash (fetchCashSummary breakdown.assets) but are not in fetchProfitLoss costs (reported as pl.assets). Excavators have their own folder (/purchases?f=veku, src/components/veku-folder.tsx): each veku is a suppliers row (material_type 'ভেকু', more can be added), hours worked are purchases item_name 'ভেকু' (quantity = hours, unit 'ঘণ্টা', unit_price = rate) and count as the cost (fetchProfitLoss costs.veku), money handed over (diesel, mobil, parts, the driver's pay) is supplier_payments with note "<kind> · চালক: <name> · …" and only offsets the bill. All vekus show as one account with a per-veku chip. Contract-folder sets include 'ভেকু', so vekus are hidden from the suppliers page and seller lists; the cash book's ভেকু tile opens the one expense form on the veku head (a payment needs its kind). Why: a long-lived purchase must not make one season look like a loss.
- AI snapshot (loadSnapshot in src/services/gemini.ts) waits for supabase.auth.getSession() and throws without a session, because RLS returns empty lists (not errors) to a logged-out query and the AI would then say nobody exists. Name lookup (findParties) also searches previous-season balances: one linked by id, or with the same name as a customer/sardar/worker, is shown under that person; job words like ম্যানেজার/বাবু never match a person alone. Worker lines carry earned/due from salary-accrual.ts like the staff pages. Why: answers must match the pages.
- Expense ledger (/ledger, খরচের খাতা, src/lib/ledger.ts fetchLedger): read only. One folder per money-out head (MONEY_HEADS order, empty ones hidden) → names → one name's lines by month. Every level shows one sum: prev (আগের বাকি: last season's opening balance and anything dated before the season start) + bill/work this season − paid this season = due now. Labour work comes from salary-accrual.ts; sellers, contract people, loans and old dues read all-time so prev carries their history; cash heads (power, misc, asset, owner withdrawal) are this season's spending with no due. A sardar's or worker's linked old due shows only in their own folder, not again under পূর্বের দেনা. Nothing is edited there: a money line links to /cash-book?row=<cash key> and a bill to /cash-book?bill=<purchase id>, where the cash book opens it (expenses and supplier payments edit in the full ExpenseEntryDialog, bills in a bill sheet, deletes need the admin password); work lines link to their own page. Why: one place to see every expense, one place to change it.
- Two kinds of advance (/advance-sales, অগ্রিম বিক্রি): ঠিক দামে বুকিং = a contracts row (contract_type yearly_fixed, contract_no "বুকিং-NNN") with contract_items (brick type, trucks, pieces_per_truck, fixed rate per piece) and its full price (trucks × pieces per truck × rate) taken at once as a collection with contract_id, all written by the RPC create_booking (admin; cancel_booking removes an untouched one with its money). One challan = one truck: on the new challan form the owner picks for each challan whether it comes from a booking (required when the customer has trucks left for that brick type; any role); a booking truck carries contract_id and is priced at the booking rate × its actual pieces; other trucks are normal sales at the day's price (paid in cash through collections, or taken from advance money). Approval locks the rate and refuses a booking with no trucks left. অগ্রিম টাকা = a plain collection (contract_id null) and the challan later takes the day's price. Accounts stay apart: the customer's normal account, the AI snapshot customers and the collections ledger use contract_id null only; a booking's account is paid − delivered value (lib/bookings.ts fetchBookings, all-time); the customer page shows both and the total; /dues has a "ঠিক দামে বুকিং" line. Hand cash counts every collection. Why: a fixed price must survive market changes, and money for bricks not yet given is owed in bricks.
