Why: the old price was fixed last season, and counting such a challan in the current account took it off twice.
- A challan at the day's price (no booking, no last season's advance) carries pay_mode cash | advance | credit (lib/customer-account.ts; null on old rows = credit), chosen on the new challan form by any role. cash: cash_received is kept as exactly one collection (cash_sale = true, sales_entry_id) by the trigger sync_cash_sale_collection: written when the challan is saved, changed with it, removed when it is rejected or deleted. Managers cannot change pay_mode/cash_received after saving (prevent_creator_field_escalation) or edit/delete that collection (RLS); the collections page and cash book show it locked. advance: the admin's price starts at the day's price (brick_types.default_unit_price, set in সেটিংস (/preferences) → দাম ও ব্যাকআপ → ইটের আজকের দাম) less customer_price_terms.discount_per_piece (admin-only, set on the customer page per 2000-brick truck). The customer's current account has one helper, fetchCustomerBalance. Why: hand cash must be right from a single entry, and an advance customer's discount must not reach their cash purchases.
- Loans can carry due_date (when a loan taken must be paid back, or a loan given is due back), entered in the money forms (ঋণ নিলাম / ঋণ দিলাম) and the loans page's edit dialog. lib/loan-due.ts (loanDue, fetchLoansDueSoon, Dhaka dates) drives the badge on the loans tab (/owners?tab=loans), a task in the admin home's আজকের কাজ (past or within 7 days) and its টাকা কুলাবে? figure, the nearest date on /dues and the AI's loan answer. Why: a promised repayment date must not be missed because it lived only in a note.
- The two loan heads that look alike are named by what happened: "দেওয়া ঋণ ফেরত পেলাম" (money in, a loan the kiln gave comes back) and "নেওয়া ঋণ শোধ দিলাম" (money out, the kiln repays a loan it took). When "দেওয়া ঋণ ফেরত পেলাম" is opened while loans taken are still owed, the income form shows them and a button to the repayment head (onSwitchHead; the cash book opens the expense form, elsewhere it links to the loans tab /owners?tab=loans). Why: the owner opened the wrong head to repay a loan.
- Day close (দিন বন্ধ, src/components/day-close.tsx): cash_day_closes keeps one row per day (close_date unique) with expected = the drawer purse of fetchCashSummary({ to: day }), counted, a generated difference and the reason (required when they differ by ৳1 or more). Any role closes a day, only an admin reopens (deletes) one. Nothing is booked to cover a difference, and entries of a closed day are not locked: the cash book's card shows when today's expected figure has changed since the close. printDaySheet prints the day's lines, hand cash before/after, the drawer count and a signature line. The card is on the cash book; the + sheet opens the same dialog. Why: a gap found the same evening is easy to trace, and a signed sheet replaces copying the day into the paper book.
- Audit: the database writes audit_logs itself (migration audit_triggers_customer_guard): an AFTER UPDATE OR DELETE trigger zz_audit_row_change (private.audit_row_change, security definer, errors swallowed) on the money tables, cash_transfers, cash_day_closes and customers stores who, the table, the id and the row before/after. The app no longer calls log_audit (not executable by signed-in users). customers: only an admin deletes; guard_customer_advance keeps advance_balance unless the update comes from the database's own recompute (security definer) or an admin. Why: a change or delete of money must leave a trace that the client cannot skip or forge.
- Challan quantity: sales_entries.quantity is an integer > 0 for every brick type, "অন্যান্য" included; adla is entered in whole feet and both challan forms refuse a decimal with a message. The challan edit page shows the customer's name read-only (rename on the customer page). /entries/<id>/edit is opened with the permission for /entries/new or /challans. Why: a challan must save, and one challan must not rename a customer everywhere.
- Admin home (src/components/admin-dashboard.tsx), top to bottom: the light hand-cash card (a thin brick-red edge, the number, PurseTiles with "টাকা সরানো" opening TransferDialog; আজ এসেছে/গেছে expands today's cash lines, each opening /cash-book?row=), আজকের কাজ (tasks built from the data: yesterday not closed, loans due (fetchLoansDueSoon), pending approvals, today's close after 17:00 Dhaka, today's kacha missing once production runs, a one-time bank hint while no cash_transfers exist; red first, three at a time, each dismissible for the day in localStorage), টাকা কুলাবে? (hand cash against sardar/staff/supplier/old dues from buildDues plus loans taken due within 7 days), পাওনা-দেনা as a two-column khata from buildDues with brick debts (customer advance, bookings, last season's bricks in trucks) kept out of the taka totals, এই মাসে খরচ কোথায় (cash book costs, "move" heads excluded, compared with last month up to the same day), and উৎপাদন ও বিক্রি, folded to one line until the season has kacha entries or challans, then season target (seasons.target_production), today's three steps and stock. The snapshot refreshes every 5 minutes; QuickActions show on md+ only (the phone has +); the NavGrid sits behind "সব পাতা দেখুন" on phones. Why: the home answers what needs doing, whether cash is enough, and who owes what, and shows nothing empty.
- Paper book (খাতায় তোলা): every money-out line of the cash book is copied by hand into the paper cash book; book_marks (entry_key = the fetchCashHistory id, page, checked) records it, read page by page by fetchBookMarks (src/lib/book-marks.ts). A line with no tick is "খাতায় তোলা বাকি", so every new expense from any form or the AI waits there. The admin home shows PaperBookCard right under hand cash (count and total from useUnbooked, nothing when all are ticked) and opens /cash-book?book=1, which starts tick mode at once (tick, give the page number, "তুলুন"); on the cash book the same banner sits right under the hand-cash card and tick mode hides the entry button, day close and period chips. A row rewritten in the database (moved to another table) needs its old mark copied to the new key. Why: the owner copies expenses into the paper book and must reach that list in one tap.
